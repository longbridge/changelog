### New Features

**Community & Messages**

- **Community**: Added a stock discussion panel and a community window, where you can browse the latest, hot and expert posts, and post, comment, like and repost directly.
- **Message Center**: Added a message center that groups notifications by category, including fund changes, community highlights, interactions and news, so you never miss what matters.

**Market Watching & Research**

- **Stock Detail**: Added a "Related ETFs" section that lists ETFs related to the stock; switch between long, short and passive ETFs in one click.
- **Chart**: Timeline charts mark the day's high and low; horizontal lines show their price on the price axis; the volume sub chart in history timeshare is taller and can be resized by dragging.
- **Earnings Calendar**: Filter by industry and report type, with year-over-year changes, to quickly find earnings for the sectors you follow.
- **LongbridgeAI**: The symbol you are viewing is automatically @ mentioned when you open a chat, so you no longer need to type its code.

**Watchlist & Orders**

- **Watchlist**: Import and export your watchlist as CSV / Excel, with a preview of the changes before importing, making it easy to migrate from other platforms or back up your groups.
- **Order**: Order detail is fully redesigned, showing order, fill and fee information at a glance, with the order history displayed as a timeline.

### Improvements

- Fixed an issue where the holdings table double-counted the market value and P/L of stock held partly inside an option combo.
- Fixed an issue where take-profit / stop-loss attached orders disappeared from the K-line chart and could not be dragged to change the price after the parent order filled.
- Navigate back and forward with the mouse side buttons; every standalone window can be closed with `cmd-w` / `ctrl-w`.
- After clicking "Update", download progress shows directly in the title bar and you can restart with one click when it finishes, without a progress dialog.
- The watchlist price flash is now off by default and can be turned on in Settings.
- Fixed crashes on Windows after the system wakes up and at startup on some machines; opening the app again brings up the existing window instead of starting a second instance.
