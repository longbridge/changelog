# v0.18.0-preview.1  <Badge type="warning" text="preview" />

_Release Date: 2026-07-15_

### Improvements
- In combination (multi-leg) option wide-table mode, the eligible-to-open quantity now appears to the left of the submit button; clicking it fills the order quantity in one tap and refreshes the estimated fee, making it easy to order at the maximum available quantity.
