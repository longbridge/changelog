# v0.18.2-preview.0  <Badge type="warning" text="preview" />

_Release Date: 2026-07-23_

### New Features

- **Financials**: Added "Earnings", "Revenue Breakdown" and "Shareholder Return" sections to stock financials, visualizing key report data to help users quickly assess company fundamentals.
- **Trade**: Added market order support for multi-leg option combos (MLEG), so combo strategies are no longer limited to limit orders.
- **Trade**: Estimated order cost now shows a before/after coupon comparison, keeping the preview consistent with the actual frozen amount — what you see is what you pay.

### Improvements

- Unified close-position to a forced second confirmation with direct submission — no more order panel popup, making the flow faster while preventing accidental orders.
- Upgraded quick order modification for "Today's Orders": quantity and price can now be typed in directly for faster edits.
- Fixed estimated amount and max buying quantity not being calculated from the trigger price for conditional market orders.
- The mispress hint popup now lists all shortcuts containing the held modifier key, with primary shortcuts shown first, making available actions easier to discover.
- Fixed visible option chain contracts not loading quotes after switching the underlying or expiry date.
- Redesigned the P&L illustrations in option strategy briefs — profit/loss zones in rise/fall colors with break-even and max profit/loss annotations, making strategy characteristics clear at a glance.
- Fixed the order depth total view no longer updating after switching to another tab and back.
- "Buy" and "Sell" in the holdings context menu are now colored with trend colors, making the action direction more distinct.
