**New:**
1. **Dream Memory:** From today, every conversation is gently remembered—and you can view or edit what it's saved about you anytime.
   - Ready from day one: Your last 30 days of chats seed its memory, so it knows you the moment you switch it on.
   - Three layers of understanding: Raw fragments are distilled into concrete facts, then condensed into high-level summaries—getting to know you from the surface down.
   - Always improving: New details are captured after each chat; every night it revisits your "dreams" to correct old facts and write fresh summaries, so it gets sharper the more you use it.
   - You say it, it updates: Correct its memory in plain language, just like chatting—no settings to dig through.
   - Remember, and lets go: After 90 days, trivial details fade away to make room for what matters.
2. **Your data, your call:** A new training toggle puts you in control—decide in Settings whether your conversations can be used to train the model.

**Improvements:**
1. **View campaigns without leaving your chat:** Tap a campaign link and the page opens right in a side drawer.
2. **Subscription benefits made clear:** Each benefit now shows a description and a detailed link, so you can see exactly what you're getting.

**Fixes:**
1. In Explore agents, the "Others" section now loads automatically as you scroll to the bottom—no more manual paging.
2. Reward notifications arrive faster, and subscription quotas are no longer reset by mistake.
3. Fixed an issue where AI-generated files (Artifacts) could fail to upload in some cases.
4. Signal-code keyword matching is now case-insensitive for more accurate hits.
5. Fixed workspace display and back-navigation glitches when opening an Agent's settings page via a direct link.
