## New Features

- **Message Center**: Added a message center entry to the title bar. Notifications are gathered in a standalone window, and order messages open the order details directly; Longbridge web pages opened from a message sign you in automatically, so there is no need to enter your account again.
- **Stock Details**: Added an "ETF" tab that lists ETFs and leveraged and inverse ETFs related to the stock, with a market filter; leveraged and inverse ETF details also show the stock they track, making it easy to compare their moves.
- **Watchlist**: Added watchlist import and export via CSV / Excel, with a per-group preview of the changes before importing, making it easy to migrate from other platforms or reorganize in bulk.
- **Earnings Calendar**: Added industry and type filters and year-over-year figures, so you can find the earnings of companies you follow faster.
- **Chart**: Timeline charts now mark the day's high and low; horizontal drawings show their price on the price axis for easier reading.
- **LongbridgeAI**: The input box automatically @ mentions the symbol you are viewing, so you can ask right away.
- **Navigation**: Added support for mouse back / forward buttons, making it easier to move between pages.

## Improvements

- The title bar update entry is now an icon, with colors distinguishing downloading, installing, pending restart and failed states; clicking "Pending restart" restarts right away to finish the update.
- "About" and "Release Notes" now open in standalone windows and can be viewed without signing in.
- Fixed an issue where attached take-profit / stop-loss orders disappeared from the candlestick chart and could not be dragged to change price after the parent order was filled.
- Fixed possible crashes after Windows wakes from sleep, at startup on some Windows devices, and when the strategy editor parses a script.
