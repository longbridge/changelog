## Improvements

- Fixed an issue where account settings (such as the currency preference on the assets page) never took effect once trade-password protection was enabled.
- Fixed an issue where a panel sitting alone in a tab group (such as Profit & Loss Analysis) could stay blank and load no data in custom split layouts or after restoring the previous layout.
