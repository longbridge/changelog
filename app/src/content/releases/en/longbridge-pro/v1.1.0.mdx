## New Features

- **Market**: Added an earnings calendar that shows upcoming earnings releases from major companies, so you don’t miss key reports.
<Video src="https://assets.lbkrs.com/uploads/2ae1441a-5b12-4a43-8c4d-220034fcaa92/Earning.mp4" label="Market: Added an earnings calendar that shows upcoming earnings releases from major companies, so you don’t miss key reports." />
- **Trade**: HK and SG stocks now support odd lot orders, so you can trade shares below one board lot directly; amount-based orders also work with odd lot orders.
<Video src="https://assets.lbkrs.com/uploads/3c8e96a0-a39e-41e4-a29e-315442a2738a/ODD.mp4" label="Trade: HK and SG stocks now support odd lot orders, so you can trade shares below one board lot directly; amount-based orders also work with odd lot orders." />
- **Trade**: Commission-free cards, platform fee cards and stock cash coupons now distinguish between US trading sessions, so you can use the matching coupons for pre-market option orders too.
- **Auto Update**: New versions now download and install automatically in the background, and the title bar prompts you to restart once ready, without interrupting your work; you can turn this off in Settings > General > Software Update.

## Improvements

- **Trade**: When a holding includes fractional shares, conditional orders can be placed and modified with fractional quantities, and clicking the sellable position quantity sells the entire position.
- Clicking a sidebar entry such as Market or Custom again now takes you back to the sub page you last visited, instead of always returning to the home page.
- **Trade**: The option short sell notice and the chart batch cancel confirmation now offer “Don’t remind me again”; check it with one click to skip future confirmations, and turn it back on anytime in Settings.
