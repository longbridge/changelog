## New Features

- **Community**: Added a Discussion panel to stock details, where you can see the latest, hot and expert posts on the stock, and post, comment, like and repost directly; a new Community entry in the sidebar lets you browse the community feed in a standalone window.
- **Order Details**: Redesigned so that order, fill and fee information is clear at a glance, with the order history shown as a timeline.
- **Notifications**: Added the Notifications center, which groups notifications by category such as fund changes, community highlights and news, so you won't miss important updates.

## Improvements

- **Chart Trading**: When you set a take-profit / stop-loss through an attached order or by dragging the position line, US stocks now default to a limit order, with the limit price set to the trigger price by default.
- **Watchlist**: Price flash is now off by default; you can turn it on in Settings.
- **App Update**: Clicking "Update" now downloads in the background, with progress shown on the title bar; clicking "Restart to Update" on the About page restarts right away without a confirmation.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue where the take-profit / stop-loss entry did not appear right after opening chart trading.
- Fixed an issue where the holdings table double-counted the market value and P/L of stock held in a combo with options.
